About the Program

The BS Computer Science program at North Negros College equips students with strong foundations in programming, algorithms, data structures, and computing theory. Our curriculum blends academic rigor with hands-on projects, preparing graduates for careers in software engineering, data science, cybersecurity, and IT innovation.

  • CHED-recognized program
  • Modern computer laboratories
  • Industry-linked internships (OJT)
  • Capstone project & research training
